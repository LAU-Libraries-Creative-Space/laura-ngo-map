1. Purpose of the Platform

This web application is an informational directory developed by the Lebanese American University (LAU) to help individuals and organizations identify and contact non-governmental organizations (NGOs) that may accept food donations. LAU's role is strictly limited to providing a directory of NGOs and their available contact information. LAU does not collect, receive, store, transport, distribute, coordinate, or supervise food donations and is not a party to any donation arrangement.

2. Donor Responsibilities

Before making any donation, donors must contact the selected NGO directly to:

Confirm that the NGO is currently accepting food donations and verify the types, quantities, and conditions of food accepted.

Verify the NGO's exact location, operating hours, and delivery arrangements.

Ensure that donated food meets applicable food safety requirements.

All donation arrangements must be made directly between the donor and the NGO, independently of LAU.
